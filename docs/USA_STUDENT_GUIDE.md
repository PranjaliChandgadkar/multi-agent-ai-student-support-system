# USA student support guide

## Audience and scope

This version supports international students planning academic study in the United States, initially focused on F-1 questions. It is not a guide for US citizens applying abroad. Career support remains available.

## What you can ask now

- What documents should I prepare for a US F-1 visa interview?
- What information should I have ready for DS-160?
- What is the role of Form I-20 in the application?
- How can I organise truthful explanations of my study plans and funding for a visa interview?

Ask one complete question and specify your visa category, where you are applying and your stage in the process when relevant. Do not enter passport numbers, SEVIS IDs, financial account details or copies of personal documents. The application does not need those identifiers to provide general guidance.

## How to read an answer

Visa responses should display source IDs and expandable citations. Open the official page, check its current content, and compare it with the excerpt. A review date records when this project's summary was checked; it is not the policy's effective date or a guarantee that the page remains unchanged. An uncited abstention means the app could not support an answer.

## Coverage boundaries

The initial pack contains short summaries of two State Department pages, not their complete contents. The application can abstain even on a US topic. Current fees, appointment availability, processing times, nationality-specific restrictions, interview waivers, travel/re-entry, maintaining status, CPT, OPT and STEM OPT are not covered by this pack. J-1, M-1, dependent visas and immigration case strategy require separately reviewed guidance. The app must not infer these rules from F-1 application information.

## Interview practice

Use the app to organise your own experiences and questions. It should not supply fabricated finances, academic history or scripted claims about your intentions. Visa interview preparation belongs to Visa Support; employment interview preparation belongs to Career Support.
